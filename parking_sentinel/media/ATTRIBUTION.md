# Demo traffic video

`demo-traffic.mp4` is a 60-second excerpt from **Cars driving at night**, by
[Editor](https://www.youtube.com/user/Editor), published May 28, 2011.

- Source: [Wikimedia Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cars_driving_at_night.webm)
- Original publication: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ina7KMV2OEI
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- Changes: first 60 seconds, resized to 640 × 360, converted to H.264 at 15 fps, audio removed.
- No endorsement by the original creator is implied.

This is public road footage, not footage from the project owner's camera.
It demonstrates real frame-based motion triggering. It is not an annotated
parking-enforcement dataset and does not establish recognition accuracy.
